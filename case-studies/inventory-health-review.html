SKU-level results

Reorder point = ceiling((annual demand ÷ 365) × supplier lead time in days + safety stock). The code uses a simple heuristic; actual policies may use service levels, variability, case-pack sizes and supplier constraints.

Manager-ready interpretation

  • Review SKU-002: sample stock (8) is below the simple reorder point (9). Confirm current open orders and supplier availability before raising a purchase order.
  • Review SKU-004: sample stock (4) is below the calculated point (7), but demand is seasonal and declining in the final months. Check upcoming events and remaining shelf life before replenishing.
  • SKU-001: on-hand (18) is just above the calculated point (17). Monitor sales and lead-time changes rather than treating this estimate as an automatic order.
  • SKU-003: stock is well above the simple point. Check expiry dates and storage constraints; high stock alone does not prove overstock.
These conclusions are calculations from the fictional CSV and its stated formula. They are not real-world business findings or purchase instructions.
