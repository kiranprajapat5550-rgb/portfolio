Safety boundaries

  • Review the exact action, target and content before deciding.
  • High-risk requests should be escalated and not approved solely on an AI recommendation.
  • A production system would need authenticated identities, server-side authorization, expiring approvals, idempotency, protected logs, tests and least-privilege credentials.
  • This prototype implements none of those production integrations and has no credentials or external execution path.
