Digital Marketing Campaign Lab

Plan a campaign, add sample performance figures and compare results with transparent formulas. Runs locally in this browser.

Demo only: The starter rows contain fictional data, not actual campaign results. No ad accounts, pixels, analytics services or external APIs are connected. Currency is INR (₹); enter figures from your own report only when you have permission.

How to use this in a portfolio

  1. Define the objective and one primary KPI before launch.
  2. Record spend, impressions, clicks, conversions and attributed revenue from a permitted report.
  3. Compare campaigns only when dates, attribution settings and conversion definitions are comparable.
  4. Write a short observation and a next test; do not assume that correlation proves causation.

This lab does not forecast performance, fetch live data, recommend spending, or guarantee results.
