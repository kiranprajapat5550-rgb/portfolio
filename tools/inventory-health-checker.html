Rules used

  • Out of stock: quantity is zero or below.
  • Low stock: quantity is below reorder level, but above zero.
  • Expiry review: expiry date is today or within the next 90 days.
  • Past expiry: expiry date is before today.
  • Slow movement: last movement date is at least 90 days ago.

These are simple screening rules, not a substitute for your organization's inventory, expiry, or compliance policy.
