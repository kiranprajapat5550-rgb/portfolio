Method notes

How to use the analysis

  • ABC ranks items by annual usage value (annual demand × unit cost).
  • XYZ classifies demand consistency from monthly sample demand.
  • Reorder point = average daily demand × lead time + safety stock; this demo uses a fixed safety-stock allowance per SKU.
  • Naïve backtest uses the previous month's demand as forecast; WAPE = sum absolute errors ÷ sum actual demand.
  • Small samples can produce misleading segments. Use a longer history, returns/censoring checks, seasonality and a business-approved service level before real decisions.
