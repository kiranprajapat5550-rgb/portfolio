My design workflow

  1. Clarify the audience, platform, message and desired action.
  2. Choose one headline, a short supporting line and a clear visual hierarchy.
  3. Build a consistent layout and check contrast and phone readability.
  4. Adapt the approved direction into platform-specific sizes and export formats.

Tools in this skill area: Canva workflows and Figma layout practice. This page does not claim that these mockups were created inside either app.

Portfolio honesty: these are concept mockups for skills demonstration, not evidence of client work or live campaign results. Before publishing a real brand asset, check brand permissions, text accuracy, platform dimensions and accessibility.
