Inventory Health Report

A simple way to identify stock exceptions that need an operations review.

Demo notice: Product names, quantities and dates below are synthetic examples only.

Illustrative exception list

ItemStockSignalSuggested check
Item Alpha8 unitsLow stockCheck recent demand and reorder level
Item Beta42 unitsExpiry reviewVerify batch and expiry dates
Item Gamma0 unitsOut of stockConfirm supplier lead time and open orders
Item Delta96 unitsSlow movementReview sales velocity and purchasing plan

Recommended report fields

  • SKU, product, category and current quantity
  • Minimum stock, average daily demand and supplier lead time
  • Batch/expiry details where applicable
  • Last movement date and stock adjustment history
  • Priority, recommended follow-up, assigned owner and review status

Review workflow

  1. Validate source data and duplicate SKUs.
  2. Flag low, zero, slow-moving or expiry-sensitive items.
  3. Have an authorized person review reorder or stock actions.
  4. Record the decision and next review date.

Static concept only. No inventory system is connected and no stock changes are made.
