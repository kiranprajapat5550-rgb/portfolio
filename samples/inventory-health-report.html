Inventory Health Report

An inventory reporting project to identify stock exceptions that need an operations review.

Inventory exceptions to monitor

SignalWhat to reviewSuggested follow-up
Low stockCurrent quantity vs. reorder levelReview demand and replenishment timing
Expiry reviewBatch and expiry datesVerify stock rotation and local policy
Out of stockAvailability and open ordersConfirm supplier lead time
Slow movementSales velocity and last movementReview purchasing plan

Recommended report fields

  • SKU, product, category and current quantity
  • Minimum stock, average daily demand and supplier lead time
  • Batch/expiry details where applicable
  • Last movement date and stock adjustment history
  • Priority, recommended follow-up, assigned owner and review status

Review workflow

  1. Validate source data and duplicate SKUs.
  2. Flag low, zero, slow-moving or expiry-sensitive items.
  3. Have an authorized person review reorder or stock actions.
  4. Record the decision and next review date.

The page outlines the report structure and review workflow. Further implementation and any data-source integration will be documented as the project develops.
