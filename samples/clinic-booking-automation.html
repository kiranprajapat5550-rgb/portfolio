Clinic Booking Automation

A clinic appointment workflow project designed to reduce repetitive coordination while keeping clinic staff in control.

Project focus: Booking requests, availability checks, staff confirmation, reminders and status tracking.

Try the appointment tracker

A browser-only prototype for adding booking references, checking overlapping appointment slots, tracking request status and exporting a schedule CSV.

Open Clinic Appointment Tracker →

Use non-identifying test entries only. This prototype does not connect to clinic calendars, send reminders or store records after the page is closed.

Proposed workflow

1. Request received
Capture only necessary booking details through an approved channel.
2. Validate availability
Check the clinic's approved calendar and flag conflicts for staff review.
3. Confirm with staff
Require authorized confirmation before an appointment is finalized.
4. Send approved reminder
Use a consented contact method and clinic-approved message template.
5. Track status
Record pending, confirmed, rescheduled or cancelled status with an audit trail.

Safety and privacy considerations

  • Collect the minimum necessary personal data.
  • Use access controls and an approved retention policy.
  • Do not place sensitive health details in general-purpose notifications.
  • Provide a manual path for exceptions and urgent questions.

A browser-only appointment tracker prototype is available. Live clinic-system integrations, reminders and secure production storage are not configured.
