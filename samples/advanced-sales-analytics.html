Questions this report should answer

  • Which products and categories contribute most to revenue?
  • How do sales vary by week, channel and region?
  • Are returns or discounts affecting net sales?
  • Which changes deserve review before the next reporting period?

Suggested dashboard structure

  1. Top-line KPIs: revenue, orders, average order value and returns.
  2. Time trend: weekly net sales with prior-period comparison.
  3. Breakdowns: category, product, channel and region.
  4. Action table: unusual changes, owner, recommended check and status.
